Questions? Answers.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How long does it take to set up?
Most businesses are live in under 30 minutes. Fill in your business details, upload a few products, and your page is ready. If you don't have a logo or product photos, our team can help you create them in 24–48 hours.
Do I need my own website or domain?
No. We give you a free page at brandinn-business.vercel.app/p/your-business-name. You can share this link on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or anywhere else. If you have your own domain later, you can connect it.
How do I receive orders and payments?
Customers can order directly on your page and pay via mobile money or cash on delivery. You'll see every order in your dashboard. You can also enable card payments by connecting a payment provider.
What if I sell services instead of products?
Perfect — BrandInn works for services too. Fitness clubs, restaurants, salons, and consultants all use BrandInn. You can list services, accept bookings, and manage schedules from the same dashboard.
What if I need help?
WhatsApp us anytime. Real people answer, usually within an hour during business hours.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. If you cancel, your page stays live until the end of your billing period, then goes offline.
Is my data safe?
All your data is encrypted in transit and stored on enterprise-grade infrastructure. We never sell your information to third parties. Only you can see your customers and orders.
