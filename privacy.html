Privacy Policy
BrandInn Business — Professional pages & online shops

BrandInn Business helps businesses — from restaurants and pharmacies to grocery stores, retail shops, service providers, and more — create a professional online presence with a business page and online shop.

By creating an account, subscribing, or using any BrandInn Business service, you trust us with your information. We are committed to protecting that trust. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Email address & name: When you register, we collect your email address and the name you provide. This information is used only for account management, password resets, and essential service communications.
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymous data about how you interact with our site (pages visited, features used) to improve our services. This data does not identify you personally.
  • Payment information: All payments (whether subscription or one‑off) are processed by secure third‑party gateways (e.g., M‑Pesa, card processors). We do not store or have access to your payment details.
  • Business & order data: We collect the business information you choose to publish (name, tagline, location, products, prices) and order details needed to operate your shop (customer name, items, delivery address). This is necessary to provide the Service.
  • Sensitive information: We explicitly ask you not to provide any unnecessary sensitive personal data (such as national ID numbers, financial details, or medical records) via forms or account profiles. Where absolutely required by law, we will collect it through secure, dedicated channels with explicit consent.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, verify your identity, and provide customer support.
  • To send you important updates about your account, subscription, orders, service changes, or security alerts. (You may also receive occasional product news – you can opt out of non‑essential emails.)
  • To operate your business page and online shop, including processing orders and enabling discovery in the BrandInn directory.
  • To analyse and improve the performance and user experience of our website and services.
  • To protect the security and integrity of our platform and prevent fraud or abuse.

Sharing & disclosure

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We may share necessary information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website, processing payments, or conducting our business – provided they agree to keep your information confidential.
  • When a customer places an order through your shop, we share the necessary order details with you so you can fulfil it. We may also share limited data with delivery or payment partners as needed to complete the transaction.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BrandInn Business, our users, or others.

Data retention & security

  • We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide you services. If you close your account, we will delete your information within a reasonable timeframe, unless we are required to retain it for legal or legitimate business purposes (e.g., transaction records).
  • We implement strong security measures including encryption, secure servers, and strict access controls to protect your information. We continuously work to safeguard your data and do everything we can to ensure customer protection.

Your rights & choices

  • You can access and update your account information (name, email, business details) at any time through your dashboard settings.
  • You may request deletion of your account by contacting us. Note that some information may be retained as necessary for legal or operational reasons (e.g., past transaction records).
  • You can opt out of non‑essential emails by using the unsubscribe link in those emails.

Cookies & tracking

  • Our website uses minimal cookies (small text files) to remember your login status and preferences. These are essential for the site to function. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.
  • We may use anonymous analytics tools to understand aggregate usage patterns – this data cannot be used to identify you.

Children's privacy

BrandInn Business is intended for business owners and representatives who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with data without your consent, please contact us immediately so we can address the situation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any material changes via email or a prominent notice on our site. Continued use of our services after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
